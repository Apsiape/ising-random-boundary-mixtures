\section{How this work was done}
Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.2 Sol and GPT-6.2 Astra assisted with exploration, proof development and mathematical checking. The proofs were written in full. The argument of the present version, valid at every $K>K_c$, was found with the assistance of AI models. It was checked by three non-author audits during its development and then by two independent refutation-first checks, by models from more than one developer in all, which compared the imported statements with their sources and hypotheses. Enumerations over all spin configurations of boxes with up to nine sites, for all boundary words or samples of them and at several temperatures, confirmed the identities of Lemmas~\ref{lem:sources} and~\ref{lem:deletion}, the inequality \eqref{eq:arcratio} for single curves, the inequality \eqref{eq:split}, and the conditional law of Lemma~\ref{lem:openlaw}. An integer enumeration of all edge sets of one dual rectangle confirmed the local contour rules and the anchoring of constrained sites to the collar. A similar enumeration on a box of twenty sites confirmed the response bounds of Lemma~\ref{lem:response} and the derivative bound in the proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:buffer}. The weights in these enumerations were evaluated in floating point, and no proof depends on them. The authors take responsibility for every claim.
