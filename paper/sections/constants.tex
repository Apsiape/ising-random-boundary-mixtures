\section{Scales and constants}\label{sec:constants}
The temperature enters the proofs through the published inputs of Section~\ref{sec:model} and through two strict inequalities,
\[
 K^*=\operatorname{artanh}e^{-2K}<K_c\qquad\text{and}\qquad m>0,
\]
both valid at every $K>K_c$. The first gives the decay rate $c_\tau>0$ of \eqref{eq:dualdecay} and hence $c_0>0$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:arcs}. The second gives the phase gap in \eqref{eq:gap} and the threshold $m/2$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:majority}. At finite $K$ the heat-bath increment $\kappa_K$ and the response factor $1-e^{-4K}$ are positive. No numerical threshold above $K_c$ enters the recurrence, lower-bound or empirical-law proofs. For the sharp upper bound in Section~\ref{sec:sharp}, the walk sum requires $K>\log2$, equivalently $\beta_{\rm w}=2K-\log4>0$. Its deletion rate $c_{\rm del}$, the macroscopic-arc rate $c_{\rm mac}$ and the majority rate $c_{\rm maj}$ have different names and roles.

Four scales appear. Near-fair biases satisfy $|p-1/2|\le n^{-2/5}$, so the mean odds in Lemma~\ref{lem:drift} grow like $n^{3/5}$. The interval cutoff of Lemma~\ref{lem:ratios} is $n^{7/10}$. The majority square has radius $k_n=\lfloor n^{4/5}\rfloor$. The cutoff for macroscopic arcs is $n/10$. The proofs use the comparisons
\[
 \tfrac25<\tfrac12,\qquad
 \tfrac35<\tfrac7{10},\qquad
 \tfrac7{10}<\tfrac45<1.
\]
The first places the tilted block laws $q_\pm=1/2\pm A/\sqrt n$ of Proposition~\ref{prop:oddswindow} inside the permitted bias range. The second makes the conditional mean $36Kn^{3/5}$ of an interval comparison negligible against the cutoff $n^{7/10}$, which leaves the disorder exponent $n^{2/5}/(288K^2b^2)$ in \eqref{eq:H}. In the third, the majority normalization $e^{n^{7/10}}$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:ratios}(iii) is beaten by the wrong-majority error $\delta_n=C_{\rm maj}e^{-c_{\rm maj}k_n}$ and by the macroscopic-arc bound $e^{-c_0n/10}$, and $k_n<n/2$ keeps the majority square inside the central component and away from the north strip.

Constants may depend on the fixed temperature and on the buffer width $b=2L+1$. For the window estimate the choices are made in the following order. First fix $K$ and the target window $(z,\eps)$. Then choose $L$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:buffer}, with activity probability $a_L=2^{-2L}$. Next choose an even depth $D\ge12$ so that each expected remainder is eventually below $a_Lm/8$, giving active expected separation at least $3a_Lm/4$. Then choose $A$ and $B_*$ from Proposition~\ref{prop:oddswindow}, and finally the lower size cutoff. In the fair anti-concentration argument a good position has gap at least $m/2$, and the depth is chosen after the desired exceptional probability.

For the sharp upper bound, fix $\theta=m/32$, choose the fixed depth $D$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:coverage}, and then set $a=\gamma_Dm/2$. The exceptional probability in Lemma~\ref{lem:manyresponses} is smaller than $n^{-1/2}$, so no depth limit is needed after the covariance estimate. For the even-side lower bound in Corollary~\ref{cor:allsides}, choose the buffer width $L$ for the target odds window, then a smaller $\theta$ and the corresponding $D$, then the drift and variance constants, and only then the fixed tilt constant $A_0$ and count-band constant $B_0$. The alternative drift proof in that corollary gives the lower bound at every $K>K_c$.
